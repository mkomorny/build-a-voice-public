HOW TO USE: build-a-voice
====================================

PURPOSE
  Design a custom offline Kokoro TTS voice: pick one of 11 base voices or
  blend two or more, save the blend as a named persona, and speak any text.

CAPABILITIES
  - Model files at B:\AI Directories_Databases\models\kokoro
    (kokoro-v0_19.onnx + voices.bin); leftover fallback C:\Users\<you>\kokoro_models
  - List voices, speak with one voice, blend by ratio, save/reuse personas
  - Speed and en-us / en-gb language hints
  - Writes a .wav you can play

USE CASES
  - Character / narrator voice for a script
  - A/B the same line in several voices
  - Reusable persona cast (narrator, villain, sidekick)
  - Proofread writing by ear

IDEAS
  - Blend af_bella:0.6,am_adam:0.4 and save as noir_detective
  - Same line at 50/50 vs 70/30 to hear the shift
  - Pair with speaking-personality-profile for a matching character

PROMPTS YOU CAN PASTE
  Build me a voice character.

  Blend a British male and an American female voice.

  Say this out loud in a detective voice: [text]

  List the voices you have.

  Save this blend as a persona named noir_detective.

EXAMPLE FLOW
  You:   Blend af_bella 60 and am_adam 40. Read: "The street was empty."
  Model: runs scripts/speak.py --blend "af_bella:0.6,am_adam:0.4" --text "..." --out out.wav
         plays or points to the wav

DEFAULT GOALS IF YOU DON'T NAME ONE
  List the 11 base voices, then speak a short test line in am_adam.
